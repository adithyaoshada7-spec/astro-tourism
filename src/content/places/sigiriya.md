## Overview

Sigiriya, often called the **Eighth Wonder of the World**, is an ancient fortress complex perched on a massive 200-meter-tall granite rock in central Sri Lanka.

## Why Visit Sigiriya?

Rising dramatically from the central plains, Sigiriya offers an incredible mix of ancient urban planning, hydraulic technology, landscaped water gardens, and 5th-century frescoes.
