## Overview

Sigiriya, often called the **Eighth Wonder of the World**, is an ancient fortress complex perched on a massive 200-meter-tall granite rock in central Sri Lanka. Built in the 5th century CE by King Kashyapa, this UNESCO World Heritage Site showcases advanced ancient urban planning, hydraulic water gardens, and exquisite artistic masterworks.

Visitors to the Cultural Triangle frequently combine an early morning climb of Sigiriya with the sacred shrines of the nearby [Dambulla Cave Temple](/places/dambulla-cave-temple), located just 25 minutes to the south. Together with southern coastal landmarks like [Galle Fort](/places/galle-fort), Sigiriya represents one of Sri Lanka's greatest architectural and historical treasures.

## Top Things to Do & See

- **Ascend through the Lion Paws:** Marvel at the gigantic carved lion paws framing the final steep staircase to the summit plateau.
- **Admire the 5th-Century Frescoes:** View the world-renowned Sigiriya Maidens, ancient cave wall frescoes that have retained their vibrant colors for over 1,500 years.
- **Explore the Water & Terraced Gardens:** Walk through one of the oldest landscaped hydraulic gardens in Asia, featuring symmetrical pools, fountains, and boulder gardens.
- **Read the Ancient Mirror Wall Inscriptions:** Stroll past the highly polished plaster wall inscribed with historical verses and graffiti left by travelers between the 6th and 14th centuries.
- **Experience 360° Summit Vistas:** Stand atop the 1.6-hectare palace ruins for panoramic views across the central plains and distant peaks.

## Insider Local Travel Tips

- **Start Your Climb Early:** Begin climbing at 7:00 AM when the ticket counters open to beat both the intense tropical heat and the tourist crowds.
- **Combine with Nearby Sacred Sites:** After descending, head directly to the [Dambulla Cave Temple](/places/dambulla-cave-temple) for an enriching cultural afternoon exploring its 150+ Buddha statues and cave murals.
- **Hydration & Footwear:** Wear sturdy walking shoes with good traction for the 1,200 metal and stone steps, and carry at least 1.5 liters of water.
- **Respect Rules & Wildlife:** Do not fly drones near the summit, and be cautious with personal items around local monkeys near the lower pathways.
